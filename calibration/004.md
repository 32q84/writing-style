# Calibration 004

Openings drafted for the Mantic cold email while developing the [Pitch](../styles/pitch.md) style, with the user's recorded verdicts. These were real drafts, not a synthetic brief. Preserve the original copy and feedback. The approved email is in the style guide.

## Shared brief

Cold email from Synoptic to Mantic, an AI forecasting company, presenting the data feed catalogue. The catalogue list was accepted early; the openings were rated.

## 004-1 | Pitch

Status: bad

> I’d like to get Synoptic’s data into Mantic. You talked about taking in as much information as possible while still being able to reproduce what was available for a backtest. We do a lot of that collection already.

User feedback: so this is cheap and shitty sales style still

## 004-2 | Pitch

Status: good

Written by the user as the replacement for 004-1.

> hi toby, wanted to reach out as a data vendor. we provide low-latency even feeds to trading firms. thought that you might be interested to take a look at our datafeed catalogue....

User feedback: supplied as the model to follow.

## 004-3 | Pitch

Status: bad

Two alternatives offered when the user asked for a better first sentence.

> Wanted to introduce Synoptic.

> I’m with Synoptic. We supply low-latency event feeds to trading firms.

User feedback: its cringe. dont you see my style, look deeper

## 004-4 | Pitch

Status: good

> We’re a data vendor supplying low-latency event feeds to trading firms. Thought you might be interested in taking a look at our data feed catalogue.

User feedback: accepted; the user later changed "supplying" to "providing" and added a sentence on pricing in live events in forecasting models.

## Other recorded corrections

- "think smart, they dont need follows unfollows, i want you to be sharp with details": X bullet reduced to real-time posts and point-in-time history.
- "govt is crammed, and loses its pitch. split what needs to be split": one government bullet became five.
- Crypto, prediction markets and AI-lab bullets removed as not relevant to the reader; compute pricing kept.
- Bold bullet labels and the closing "sample pack and history ranges to evaluate against your current research inputs" were dropped in favour of "Can send over samples or set up a call if anything looks useful."
