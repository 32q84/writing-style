Synthetic drafts with the user's recorded verdicts. Preserve the original copy and feedback. A verdict applies to that candidate, not to every technique it uses. Do not use these invented facts as claims about a real product.

## Shared brief

Write an X post for existing users of a fictional dashboard called Relay. Saved keyword searches can now send email alerts when a new result matches the saved keywords. Users enable email alerts in each saved search's settings. They do not need to keep the dashboard open to receive alerts. Existing dashboard results are unchanged. Alerts are included in every existing plan at no additional charge. No delivery-time guarantee, engagement statistics or development backstory is supplied.
