## Shared brief

Write a longer launch post for Bench, a fictional workshop opening public furniture-repair sessions. Address local adults, including beginners. Sessions run on Saturdays, 10 am–noon and 2–4 pm, with eight places per session. A place costs $20 and includes shared hand tools, basic materials and staff guidance. Visitors work on their own items alongside staff; this is not a drop-off repair service. Accept chairs, stools, drawers and other small wooden household items that one person can carry. Exclude electrical items, upholstery and large cabinets. People submit a photo and description through the website; staff confirm suitability before the visit. Any replacement parts outside the basic materials cost extra, agreed before purchase. Some items need another session or a specialist; do not guarantee a completed repair. No membership or previous repair experience is required, and the tools stay at the workshop. Do not invent a location, opening date, customer history, environmental statistics or savings claims. Aim for roughly 160–220 words per candidate. Clearly comic or hypothetical situations are allowed, but not invented customer testimonials.
